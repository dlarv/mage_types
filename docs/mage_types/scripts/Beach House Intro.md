>>>>>**Caretaker**: Please, take no offense. Us who are biased towards Blue find conversing with others difficult.

Others will cloud your mind, pollute your soul, deform and degrade you. With each transmutation, these effects will build up until, finally, you are composed of one element above all others. This is what it means to be biased.
>>**Player**: And you're Blue-biased?

>>**Player**: How many... options do we have to choose from?

>>**Player**: I mean, aren't you a little... biased. (ba dum tiss)

*The door opens.*
>**Caretaker**: Through this door and to the right, you will find the Budding Hope Enclave. They should be willing to share some of their enlightenment to help you gain your footing in this world. Good luck.
