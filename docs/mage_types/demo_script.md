## Mural Story
The murals follow the story of 4 Godlings: Redchild, Bluechild, Greenchild, and Yellowchild. The act of creating their children was an iterative process for the Old Ones. Because of this, there was an emphasis on being the youngest for the Godlings. For the longest time, Red, Blue, and Green were the youngest, in which they took much pride. This pride was shattered, however, with the creation of Yellowchild. Unable to cope with their jealousy, Redchild and Greenchild killed them. Though not directly involved in the murder, Bluechild was sympathetic to their jealousy and ultimately sided with them.

But beings like the Godlings do not simply die, at least not easily. The Yellow Tower was constructed to contain the corpse of Yellowchild. Disturbed by these actions, the other Godlings went to war. Using the power from their Tower, RGB defeated their older siblings. 

But their victory was shortlived (relative to the Godling lifespan), as the corpse of Yellowchild began to stir. Fearing the retaliation that could result should Yellowchild wake up, the Godlings began building the Observatory. By siphoning off the excess energy contained within the corpse, they were able to catch a glimpse of superspace. Obsessed with this discovery, they quickly began the construction of the Portal Complex, which also siphoned off of Yellowchild's energy. 

Once completed, the Complex was only used twice. The first time pulled in the Power Plant (along with a few mortals, but these were mostly unnoticed by the Godlings), which clipped the Tower, without significantly damaging it. The portal proved impossible to traverse, as reality rushed in to fill the void. In denial of the machine's destructive power, the Godlings turned it on again. This time, the Parking Lot was pulled in, smashing the Observatory. Greenchild and Redchild were killed in the deluge. Bluechild, slowly dying from the now heightened reality, managed to close the rift and rendered the Complex unusable.
