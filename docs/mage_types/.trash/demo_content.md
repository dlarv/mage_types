# Attacks
Green and Purple: Hyper offense.
Purple is targeted, vindictive, focuses on damage and debuffing opponent.
Red focuses on Hp.
Magenta focuses on helping allies.
Orange focuses on transmutation and relationships with other elements.
Blue is about stasis and limiting transmutation.
Cyan is about defense and tanking.
Yellow is misc.

- Orange attack that inflicts status condition based on primary type.
- Attack that does more/less damage based on number of times BattleActor has transmuted.
Cyan, Magenta, Red, Green lean more towards melee.
Blue, Purple, Orange, Yellow lean more towards ranged.
