### Element Theming
Tentative mapping of Elements onto other elements. If this goes somewhere, add it into [[Background#Lore|The Elements]] and save this section as a #discussion.

I think it would make sense for Red, Green, and Blue to receive themes as sections of the color wheel, as well as their own identities.
 >[!note]
>I think an unspoken design principle I've been using regarding the Elements is as follows:
>- Red: Physical
>- Blue: Mental
>- Green: Emotional

Blue and Purple have Astral/Psychic, connecting to the world on a spiritual level. However, Purple is much more dark.

Cyan is definitely Ice/Steel. Temperance, resolve, determination, strength.

Red gets most of its theming from blood. It also has some traces of Ground/Plant, due to its agricultural roots.
Red is dogmatic, grounded, and stubborn.

Orange is a very artificial, tech-y type. Cassette Beast's unnatural types, especially plastic, match its vibe. Fire also makes a lot of sense.

Yellow is a very airy, light element. Air, Lighting, Light all make sense. I also think of dust floating in the wind and sandy deserts. Its a very dry type.

Green would make a lot of sense as an Earth/Poison type.
- Radioactivity.
- Crystal caves and jungle.

Magenta makes a lot of sense as a Fairy type. The aesthetics of CB's Glitter type (tho not its mechanic) are also suitable. Its a very whimsical, dreamy type. Its very surreal.

# Visual Identities
**Blue**: Water, tide pool.
**Purple**: Deep ocean, deep caves.
**Magenta**: Deep woods, carnivals, dreamscapes.
**Red**: Gardens, marshes.
**Orange**: Artifical, plastic, laboratory.
**Yellow**: Sand/desert, mist/fog.
**Green**: Crystals, rainforests.
**Cyan**: Icy mountains.

Magenta and Orange's visual identities are definitly the weakest.
