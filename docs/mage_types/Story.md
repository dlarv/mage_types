# Intro
Note: the player character's canonical name will be Alyss. The player can change this, of course, but that'll be the default name for now. 

The player comes home to their apartment from a long day at work. They look inside the fridge for something to eat, only to notice an odd takeout container. It is simply labelled "Diner: Just off the Cyan Interstate." The player character has heard of neither this generic diner nor of this interstate. They take a bite of the pancakes, noting they taste good, and head into their room. However, once crossing the threshold, they find themselves in an unfamiliar stairwell. They look around, clearly confused.
>We might have to railroad the player to ensure they open the fridge.

The player is able to explore the stair well, not that there's much to explore. There will be at most 3 levels, but likely just one. The stairs will wrap around. Maybe the other levels can be unlocked during the game and will lead to other locations on Forlorn.

Exiting the stairwell will take the player to the lighthouse, another confusing transition. The door will lock behind them, forcing them down the pier. On the beach, the player will find a caretaker, a man in the late stages of Blue-alignment, rooted in place. With him is *Alice*, the player's first companion. Alice and the player will get to know each other, exchanging 3 small talk questions. The player's responses to these questions will determine Alice's *fated bias*.

Upon asking the man about his condition, he will explain the concept of *alignment*, what it is and that it's an inevitable fact of life on Forlorn. Alice is horrified by the concept, due in no small part to the man's horrifying condition. The man suggests she and the player should expose themselves to each element, which is known to help stave off alignment, at least for a little bit. Then, if Alice really finds the idea unbearable, she should seek out *Mr. Beau*, a man who has been unaligned for almost 10 years. However, the man does not know of Mr. Beau's location, suggesting Alice seeks out someone in the Magenta Community Center.

The player character finds their mind transfixed on the diner, although whether this is due to them connecting it to their current situation or because the food was really good is up to the player. They can ask the man about either the diner or the Cyan Interstate. He says it sounds familiar, but confesses he has not been on the surface in years and his memory is foggy.

Before Alice and the player leaves, the man asks them to keep an eye out for a boy named *Alex*. Evidently, he came through here about a week ago, but seemed a little air-headed, so the man worries he has gotten lost. As the player and Alice ascend through the Blue Caverns, they do happen to stumble into Alex. Alice/Alyss asks them if they're okay, have they really been lost down here for an entire week! To which Alex replies, "no, I was only lost down here for 3 days. I finally made it to the surface, found the community center, and was chilling out around there. Then, an hour ago I walked into what I thought was a maintenance closet and found myself back here."
# Main Character Motivations
Alice wants to avoid alignment, but finds one of the elements alluring. She wants to talk to Mr. Beau to ask him for advice on avoiding this fate. She is best characterized by her fear of change, wanting to freeze everything in time even at the cost of stagnation. She has anxiety and a tendency to catastrophize. Alice is very smart and technical, studying to be an engineer in college.

Alex loves this new place he found, but is conflicted with a sense of loss for his previous life. He wants to explore, but would like Alyss & Alice's help as to not get lost (again). His disappearance to Forlorn marks the second time his efforts to set his life in order have failed, leaving him with a deep sense of fatalism. While he does his best to suppress these dark emotions, this proves difficult as the tension ramps up. Alex is searching for his purpose in the world, feeling unmoored and lost. When he learns about the Hunters, it excites him unlike anything before. But the dark hopelessness ever lingers.

The player is tasked with finding the Diner, which Alyss is obsessing over. Whether this is a whimsical adventure to taste those amazing pancakes again or a desperate attempt to get home is up to the player, via dialog options and their personal roleplaying. Alyss represents a sort of optimistic nihilism, experiencing the game as though one step removed. "This might as well be happening/What else is there to do" personified. An absurdist champion.

Alex and Alice's arcs will be about helping each other live in the moment and learning how to better cope. This will be accentuated by Alyss' calm nature.
# Major Actors
## Blue Enclaves
The Enclaves are small groups of 2-6 Blue-aligned who are rooted in place within talking distance of each other. These enclaves could give the player small quests, like finding items for them or passing messages from one enclave to the other.
## The Great Enclave(s)
The shattered remanants of the once ~50 strong Great Enclave conflict with one another, each believing their own chapter to be the true successors to the Great Enclave. There are three of these successor enclaves, which the player can choose to ally themselves with. Theoretically, the player could ally themselves with all three, as the groups have no way of verifying the player's loyalty.
## Parking Garage
The player can venture into the parking garage early into the game, although all but the first level is more of a late-game area. Here they'll have to fight off Purple-aligned, wraiths (Purple-aligned that have lost themselves), and various monsters.

On the first level, the player can enter the convenience store, meeting the Clerk and some Purple mages. The Purple mages offer the player some advice and gives them a quest concerning a generator on the second level. On this level will be a powerful monster that the player cannot tackle directly, even at a higher level, and must therefore sneak around. The mages don't actually expect you to be able to accomplish this and are impressed if you do.
## The Community Center
The main conflict in the community center has to do with the boarded up rooms, used to contain rogue monsters. These restricted areas have grown to consume half of the center's area, allowing many of these once isolated containment zones to connect, leading to a rapidly growing threat. The core leadership seems to be in denial of this fact, though their opposition (the peacekeepers) are quickly gaining support.
## The Peacekeepers
Initially a simple security force used to contain the monsters raging inside the Center, a change in leadership has taken them down a darker path. An exiled Green-aligned mage has unseated the previous chief of security and is trying to maintain his power through the chaos.
## The Gardeners
Recently a valuable medicinal plant was stolen, an action the Gardeners blame on Green. The newfound leader of the peacekeepers has stoked these concerns, hoping to exacerbate the already shaky relationship between the two, in order to cover up the true reason for their exile.
## The Rangers
The Ranger faction is primarily responsible for the security of the woods. However, they seem to be struggling in recent times, as 5 people have gone missing over the past month. #todo
## Mr. Beau
Mr. Rai N. Beau is notable for having survived almost 10 years on Forlorn without alignment. This is due, in part, to them not being the same type of entity as the rest of the alignment (similar to the player, as the player did not originate from the same world as Alice/Alex/etc).

Beau is a survivalist from a post-apocalyptic world. They are very superstitious, which they credit for their non-alignment. He is intended to be a foil to Alice. Both are, at their core, afraid. But while Beau does a better job hiding this, he's let his fear turn him selfish. This, when combined with his facade of confidence, makes him the perfect conman. i intend for him to try and use Alice to advance his own goals, only to realize the error of his ways and sacrifice himself to save the player characters. This will result in him receiving a grevious injury and becoming aligned.

The player first hears about Beau from the caretaker on the beach, where Alice becomes enamored with finding him. The player will run into him throughout their journey, doing little side quests for him. His ultimate goal is to find the Primordial Garden, where he believes he'll find the source of immortality. It is here that he will betray Alice and face his fate.
# Plot
## Main Plot

>[!note] Themes
>The major theme in this game is the corruptive and transformative nature of power. This takes the form of both the magic system, as well as power in a more mundane sense.

The player will be fairly free to explore and help the factions as they see fit. Each faction will have its own conflict/plot, allowing the player to choose sides, etc. 

The main plot will center around the conflict between Green and Magenta. There has long been resentment between the two groups, but tensions have recently flared. An exiled Green mage (*Laurel*) is stirring up resentment against the leadership of the Community Center, who haven't done much to address the backroom monsters. This marks a shift in sentiment towards Green, as their decisive action seems welcome. However, the fact that this popular mage was exiled speaks badly for the faction as a whole.

Furthermore, the Gardeners, tasked with maintaining the Greenhouse, have recently lost a valuable plant and blame Green for stealing it. Though initially it will seem like Green are the antagonists, talking with their faction will reveal the situation to be much more complicated. The faction did steal from the Gardeners, an action fuelled by a desperate need for the healing properties of the stolen plant. This need could have been dealt with diplomatically, but communication between the two factions has always bordered on hostile. As far as the Laurel is concerned, the less contact between Magenta and Green, the less likely people will be to learn of his past crimes. The Gardeners would have given the player a quest to confront the powerplant, but were forbidden to by Laurel, a fact they are rather upset about.

Gaining the trust of Green will reveal the other side of this plot. Laurel is wanted for having excessively destructive tendencies, leading to several people getting injured and property damage. Through this, the player will be exposed to the hyperbole used when talking about Green. To those unfamiliar, the faction as a whole will be seen as "excessively destructive." It is revealed that Green did in fact steal from the Gardeners, but did so to heal some of Laurel's victims.

Meanwhile, the old Peacekeeper second in command, *Rose*, is trying to overthrow Laurel and take control herself. Both Rose and Laurel try to enlist the help of the player, asking for them to ask Cyan or Red for help respectively. Depending on who the player helps, one side will come out victorious, setting their sights on Green. Canonically, it will be Rose who wins, lending to the theme of power corrupting. However, the storyline will play out fairly similarly regardless of who wins. Learning of an ancient mystical artifact, Rose (or Laurel) will begin to search for it, the paranoia and lust for power spiraling them out of control.

As their madness progresses, Rose/Laurel descend into the maintenance halls/Catacombs, searching for this weapon. The player chases after them, fighting their way through fanatical Peacekeepers (the less devout having deserted). This chase culminates in Rose/Laurel transforming into a terrible beast and fighting the player's party. 

The following factors will have an effect on the ending:
- If the player befriends Lime, Green will join the fight in the halls/Catacombs, occupying a third of the Peacekeeper fanatics. Similarly, if the player has completed the Ranger quests, they too will join the fight, occupying another third of the Peacekeepers. 
- Befriending Lime also comes with the benefit of her immense combat strength.
- If Laurel won and the player has aligned to Red or Alice's fated bias is Red, they'll have an easier time navigating the Catacombs and will beat Laurel there.
- If Rose won and the player has aligned to Purple or Alice's fated bias is Purple, they'll have an easier time navigating the maintenance halls and will beat Rose there.
- If the player has completed the Hunter quest and met Last, he will join the final confrontation, before Rose/Laurel transform. Instead of simply being unable to find the weapon, they learn that Last threw it over the side of the island.

Act 1 officially starts when the player enters the Magenta Community Center. Here, they will meet the various related factions. They'll be given a quest to venture into the forest to look for medicinal plants, so that the loss of the stolen plant can be lessened. To fulfill this quest, the player must meet with the Rangers. They are also given a quest by the Peacekeepers to do reconnaisance on a distant part of the Center. Completing these two quests will start act 2, however the player will also be given a major quest by the Rangers. The player can also reach the Diner, but will find it closed, as the Cook is catering to a party in the Tower. From here, they can technically reach Orange and Cyan, but will be significantly underleveled. But this means that they can start the Hunter subplot and find Lime. 
- If Lime is in the player's party when they try to enter the Community Center, she will leave out of anxiety, returning to the powerplant.
- The player is also able to ascend the Tower and find the Cook, but they will not return to the Diner until act 2. When the player reaches them, the party will be over, the food eaten, and the Cook 'cleaning up.'

During act 2, the player will be approached by Laurel and Rose (separately), who each gives them a quest to enlist the help of Red/Cyan. Both also mention a Green conspiracy against them and ask the player to avoid the powerplant. Entering Red or Cyan, the player is given a quest to complete before they can ask for that faction's help. Alternatively, the player can ignore Rose and Laurel, visiting Green. Here, they'll be given a task to find Lime, who is somewhere in Cyan. Completing either the Cyan or Red questline will the player to enlist their help. Only Cyan or Red can be enlisted. Lime can now join the player's party and will not leave upon entering the Center. The player should also be a high enough level to take on the Orange Campus, unlocking the Hunter subplot.
- Lime can now officially join the player's party.
- Player can complete the Ranger and Orange Campus subplots at their leisure.
- The player has full access to the map, except for the Catacombs and maintenance tunnels.
- The Cook returns to the diner, allowing Alyss to finish their personal objective.

Once the player finishes the enlistment arc and returns to the Center act 3 will begin. Here, they will find the battle has already begun. Either Cyan or Red mages will be fighting the Peacekeepers. The player makes their way to where Laurel and Rose are fighting. Laurel is giving his villain monologue, revealing the existance of a powerful artifact that will 'deal with the Green problem once and for all.'  If the player chose to help Laurel, he will then leave you to 'clean up the mess,' runnning off to the Catacombs to search for the weapon. Rose accuses you of betraying her and a boss fight begins. If instead the player helped Rose, Laurel will incapacitate her mid sentence, upon noticing the player's party, knocking her out instantly. The boss fight then begins.

The supposed location of this artifact will change based on who the player helped. If this was Laurel, he will already be at the Catacombs. Otherwise, Rose will come to and thank you for your help. She will then begin muttering about the artifact Laurel mentioned was in the Purple maintenance halls. She makes her way into a nearby closet, entering the same halls Alex previously got lost in. Several loyal Peacekeepers follow her inside. 

The player can choose when to start this final confrontation, allowing them to finish any of their other side plots. I'd prefer that the game not reset once the player finishes the main story (e.g. once the boss battle concludes, the player is sent back to their previous save).

### Missing People
The people living outside the Community Center face a different problem, albeit a monstrous one. People who have ventured into the woods have disappeared without a trace, leading to concerns that something is stalking the forest. The player isn't tasked with hunting/stopping the monster, but instead is explained the situation and told to be cautious. The monster seems to stalk groups of 1-2, so the player characters are likely safe, but better safe than sorry.

Normally, solving an issue like this would be up to the Hunters, however, the bulk of their numbers are currently deployed into the depths of the Orange Campus. A solitary hunter remains above ground, working the case. They've been having more success than the Rangers, but still not enough.

After the Hunters, responsibility would fall to either Green or Red. However, both factions seem unwilling at this time. Due to both groups tendency towards secrecy, it isn't entirely clear whether this is because they're busy or simply don't care. As the player gains the trust of both factions, they'll realize that its the latter.

The Green faction initially made some headway in finding the monster, but have since been occupied with recovering from Laurel's parting acts. Even still, they express their concern for Lime heading out alone. They suggest that if you were to find Lime, they would remember it.

Similarly, Red attempted to find the monster, but stopped due to internal conflict. The two Red subfactions are fighting for dominance, a quite frequent affair. Unlike many of the other plot lines, the player cannot get involved with this struggle, as no non-Red-aligned mages are allowed such an intimate peek inside. This does make them more willing to involve the player in their external problems, however. Where other factions would be concerned about harm falling to three inexperienced mages, Red has no such qualms.

Finally, it seems that once Cyan stepped in to fix a similar problem in the past, being the most combat capable of the Defensive colors. However, despite every other group falling through, the faction has not offered the help. Traveling into the mountains, the player will discover them to be unaware of the issues below. Instead, the player will find them obsessing over an antique [[#Cyan's Restoration Project|tank]]. All their logistical and engineering prowess has been focused solely on rebuilding this toy. This isn't out of some desire to use it against the monsters or other factions. They simply want to restore it for the fun of it.

By talking to Green, Red, and the solitary hunter, the player is able to piece together enough information about the monster. Its lair will be located in the *Northwest corner of the map*, in a creepy little cave system. The monster will be a haunted doll that slipped to Forlorn from another world. It isn't intentionally abducting people, instead they are trying to open a portal home, but cannot seem to get the other side to open outside of the island. *All people who have disappeared were last seen nearby reflective objects*.

The doll fights you on sight, only talking after you defeat them. Four of the five lost people are alive, though roughed up. The fifth, however, was somehow sent to the doll's home world... a fact that enrages the doll (b/c why this rando and not them).

### The Orange Campus
About a month or so before the player arrived on the island, an enormous explosion shook the Orange Campus. Several floors of their subterranean complex were rendered inaccessible, either physically cut off or full of dangerous chemicals. Soon, deadly monsters began to form, instantly halting any rebuilding efforts the researchers had began. The Hunters were called in to help stabilize the area and have spent the past month tirelessly working.

When the player arrives, they are cautioned to avoid an entry hatch in the forest; a back entrance to the ruined complex. They are warned about the dangerous transmutative chemicals, but make the mistake of mentioned a strange power wielded by the Last of the Old Guard. A sort of super-stasis, they call it, a power which could be used to prevent alignment. This intrigues Alice, who wants to check it out. Alex also wants to explore, borne from an avid interest about the Hunters. Everyone on the island seems to hold this organization in high regard, viewing them as heros.

If the player chooses to venture into the complex, they will find a series of puzzles and challenges. The upper levels of the area have already been stabilized by the Hunters, though the researchers have yet to move back in. Soon, the player will encounter the Hunter basecamp. Here, two quests will emerge. In acts 2 or 3, the player can help the Hunters, choosing to venture further into the complex. If the player is still in act 1, the Hunters will refuse their help, citing the player's lack of experience. Additionally, the Hunters will ask the player to run a message up to their main camp, located inside of Cyan. This quest can be accepted at any point, though it may take a bit before the player is a high enough level to safely venture into the mountains.

Also here, the player has the chance to meet the *Last of the Old Guard*. They'll have the option to mention the monster terrorizing the woods above, which leads to some colorful language from Last. He clarifies that he is not mad at the player, simply tired after 200 years of fighting monsters. Unfortunately, Last doesn't see the situation up top as a priority, determining that if it was really important, another one of the factions would take care of it.

>[!note]
>If the player finishes the Hunter's message quest but not the Missing People plot line, the latter will be locked after the player fights the final boss, as the Hunters will have solved the problem. 
### Helping the Hunters
After meeting them inside the Orange Campus, the player will be given an important message to take to their home base. They are also asked to retrieve an item and bring it back to them, although they stress that it is not urgent. This second part of the quest ties back into the second half of the Orange Campus plot line.

This hunter is not in their homebase, however, as they are investigating the forest monster. The player is capable of discovering this fact without visiting the base by encountering them in the forest. However, if the player does happen to venture into the mountains, they'll find a note telling them to check the forest.

>[!idea]
>The quest giver tells the player they can likely find the other hunter in their home base. If the player has met the other hunter however, they have the option to mention this ("really, I saw them in the forest?"), to which the quest giver will reply that they've been down here for a month and obviously doesn't have a pinpoint on their comrade's location ("I was just trying to be helpful").

The note essentially tells the hunter that the Campus crew will need an artifact from their vault, asking them to bring it down at their earliest convenience. A hasty addition to the note tells the hunter they can outsource this to the player ("Don't worry, if they try and run off with it we'll find them"). When the player gives the hunter the note, they'll receive a locker key in return. This key will only open a specific locker, so in the worst case scenerio, the player could only steal one artifact.

Once the player has the artifact in their inventory, this specific quest is considered completed. Technically, the actual quest will not be checked off until the player returns to the hunter's campus base, but they will not be expected to return until they're ready to finish the 2nd half of the Campus quest. In other words, the technical end of the Helping the Hunters quest is also the beginning of the 2nd half of The Orange Campus quest.
