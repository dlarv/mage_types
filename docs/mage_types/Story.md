# Outline
## Main Characters
**Alice**:
- Hates the idea of alignment, but falls in love with a particular element.
	- Interested in the more chemical aspects of the world.
- Studying to be an engineer.
- Adventurous.
- Bad homelife, but dislikes Forlorn.

**Alex**:
- Loves the idea of alignment, but can't decide on a particular element.
- Nerd, loves learning about the island.
	- More interested in history and such lore.
- Risk adverse.
- Home life had just taken a turn for the better, but also loves Forlorn.
## Other Major Characters and Entities
**Newton Foundation**
- Research facility, claiming to be searching for a way off the island.
	- Has been corrupted from the inside, is now actually trying to develop a superweapon.
- Studies ancient, lost elements.
	- An old story tells of an element that would allow its user to leave Forlorn.
- Has been spreading misinformation about Green, blaming their crimes on the region.
	- Green discovered their secret base, but held onto the information for too long.

**Red Priesthood**
- Suspects the Foundation of lying about Green.
	- Doesn't particularly care about Green, they moreso hate being lied to.
	- Suspicions began when the Foundation recruited a Red-aligned person, who immediately acted like a double agent.
	- Been using underhanded and aggressive tactics to investigate. Implied to have tortured a Foundation member (Frank).

**Frank**
- High ranking Foundation member, knows the truth about the organization?
- Being targeted by Red.
	- Player helps them escape Red, before learning about the truth.

**Lime**
- Earnest Green-aligned person.
- She wants to defend Green's reputation.
	- Travelled South to Blue to talk to the leaders of the Union.
	- Got turned away, is now travelling North, parallel to the Player.

**Ms. White**
- Foundation member, but doesn't know about the weapon project.
- Has the record for going the longest without forming an alignment.
	- Alice really looks up to her, wanting to follow in her footsteps.
- After the Foundation is revealed to be the true villains, sacrifices herself to save the Player and friends.
	- She doesn't die, just develops a bias, due to sustaining a significant injury.
## Story Line
![[Background#Intro]]
### Blue
- Player solves the Blue Temple, using the Stasis spell.
- Alice would be interested in this spell, as she would see its potential to stave off alignment.
- Alex would be more interested about how the temple was actually built by Cyan.
- He'd also find the spiritual/psychic aspects of the element interesting.

>[!note] Lime
>Either on the way to the Blue Temple, or on the way from the temple to Purple, the player will meet Lime.
>- She'll ask what they know about Green.
>	- Alex will reply about them being aggressive, which she will take some offense to.
### Purple
- Player visits the surface enclave, with the option to visit more.
	- Player learns about resistances and weaknesses.
- Player solves the Purple Temple, using the Destroy spell.
- Alice would find the resistance mechanic interesting, while Alex (who is less enthusiastic about combat) would find it unpleasant.
- Alex would explain how Blue and Purple are two sides of the same coin.
### Magenta
- Player explores the theme park.
- Player meets Frank and the Red Investigators.
	- Frank will seem nice.
	- The Investigators will be super shady.
- Both Alice and Alex would be charmed by the fun exterior of the region.
- But when they discover the backrooms, Alex will be largely apprehensive (morbidly curious?), while Alice would want to explore.
	- Frank breaks into the backrooms to escape the Investigators and Lime will follow.
	- Player follows to check on Lime and partly because Alice wants to explore.
	- Player meets Ms. White back here.
		- She came to help out Frank.
		- Alice is super excited to meet her and wants to learn more about how to stay unaligned.
		- Ms. White indulges Alice and the player helps her track down Frank.
	- The Magenta staff would not like this area being explored.
		- Frank/White/Foundation will blame this on Lime, but the player can choose to back her up.
			- The player think that Frank/White is just confused, not malicious.
			- Alice will likely be more inclined to help out White than Lime, but it will be the player's choice.
### Red
- Red-aligned aren't particularly interested in the group, unless they're interested in joining their alignment.
- Player solves Red Temple using the Vines spell.
- Alex likes their recovery and support spells, but is less enthused about their combat.
- Alice is mostly only interested if her fated bias is Red.
- Player runs into Frank again, who is cornered by the Investigators.
	- Investigators vs player & Frank
	- Player starts to lose.
	- Lime joins the fight and helps you defeat the Investigators.
		- With her on your side, the fight is leagues easier.
- Ms. White catches up right as battle ends.
	- Frank seems reluctant to thank Lime, but Ms. White does so with no problem.
### Orange
- Player explores the research facility.
- Alice would be in good company, as both her and the Orange-aligned would be scientifically minded.
- Alex would find this place the most boring.
	- He doesn't seem like he would like the color Orange?
### Yellow
- Player solves the Sunset Temple, using the Catalyst spell.
- Alex would like Catalyst.
- Alice would be more confused on why overworld transmutations require a specific spell. Alex would be unable to give her an adequate explanation.
- The Yellow-aligned are less interested in the Player, as they cannot enter their air-borne city.
### Green
- Green is currently hostile, due to their recent confrontations with Cyan and the Foundation.
	- The character's previous interactions with Lime would make them less hostile.
- Player solves the Green Combat Trial.
	- After finishing this Trial, the player is given access to the Abandoned Temple.
- After an endorsement from Lime and finishing the Trial, the player is pulled into the conflict.
	- Lime travelled to Northern Island and the rest of the Green-aligned are freaking out.
	- Player travels to island to help.
		- Green don't want you and your companions to charge into danger, they tell you to leave the fighting to them. They simply believe you will have an easier time convincing her to return.
		
>[!important]
>At this point of the story:
>- Player must be friends with Lime.
>- Neither Player nor Lime fully understand/know that the Foundation is the real villain.
>	- Green leadership didn't tell Lime, because they didn't think she needs to know.
>	- Lime fully believes she can talk things out.
### Northern Island
- Foundation is revealed to be villains.
- Player helps Lime fight her way back to safety.
- Player confronts leader of Foundation.
- After the battle, Green and Red secure the island, kicking the Foundation out.
### Cyan
- Frank attacks player, angry at the defeat of the Foundation.
	- Ms. White saves the player, injuring herself in the process and becoming Cyan-aligned.
- Player solves the Cyan Temple, using all the spells they've unlocked.
- Similar to Orange, Alice would be really intrigued by Cyan and their engineering prowess.
	- Alice's canonical fated bias would probably be Cyan, with Purple being a close second.
- Player finishes Quest.
### Southern Islands
- Player returns to Blue.
	- If player enters townhall, they'll have the ability to see the aftermath of the Foundation.
- Player can venture out to the Southern Islands (postgame area).
	- Battle Gauntlet.
